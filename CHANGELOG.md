# Changelog

All notable changes to CuteCraft Studio. Format: newest first.

## Unreleased

### Fixed

- The glitter eyeshadow (and its sparkles) now follows the shape of the eye: it sits on the actual lid and tilts with the eye, e.g. rises towards the outer corner on cat eyes.

- Skirts no longer stick out at the hips under tops, and the ruffle dress (and its petticoat) now widens smoothly from the waist instead of denting in and bulging out at the hips.
- The figure keeps its size when the head accessory, hairstyle or outfit changes (bunny ears used to shrink it). Figures are a little smaller overall, so even the tallest accessories fit.

### Changed

- Saving a character no longer suggests an automatic name like "Cutie 1": the name field stays empty unless you type a name (unnamed characters show as "Unnamed" in the gallery).
- Doe eyes are rounder (no boxy corners) and have no eyelid lines; the eyeliner is now a clean line that follows the lid and ends in a small soft wing.
- Eyelashes (all styles, upper and lower) look more natural: fewer, curved, tapered lashes of varied length that lean towards the outer corner; lower lashes are short and sparse.
- The oval face is a little wider.

### Added

- New hairstyle "Messy bun": a puffy, loose bun on top of the head with a few soft loose pieces (also in the random generator).
- More clothes: one-shoulder, cut-out (heart) and halter tops; summer, sheath, maxi and ruffle dresses (they replace the bottom, like the dress); leggings and baggy pants; fishnet tights and sheer black tights (the sheer tights are always black, the fishnet follows the sock colour). Black was added to the clothes colours. The random button uses them too.
- Colour wheel: the palette button (🎨) next to every colour row now opens a colour wheel to pick the hue and saturation, plus a brightness slider.
- More eye options: voluminous lashes, optional lower lashes, eyeliner (a crisp dark line with a longer wing), and earthy or glitter eyeshadow (with a choice of glitter colour). Eyeshadow also works on happy (closed) eyes.
- More glasses: thin wire, square and cat-eye frames, plus sunglasses (round, aviators, heart and star). The frame colour can be chosen for all glasses, the lens colour for sunglasses.

### Changed

- Side ponytail: it now grows out of a gathered point at the side of the head (the hair tie is visible) and falls in a soft curve with more volume, tapering to a soft tip.
- Long, wavy and hime hair: the two front locks hang straight down, evenly wide and without the narrowing twist.
- Removed all baby hairs and single stray hairs around the head, the tails and the back hair (the optional ahoge and the curl textures stay).
- Legs look more natural: fuller thighs that taper to a soft knee, a gentle calf and slim ankles, and the legs stand a little closer together at the top. Skirts, shorts, trousers and socks follow the new shape.
- Sailor blouse: the collar now lies on top of the shoulders and sleeves instead of disappearing behind them.
- Long hair: the two side locks now fall straight down beside the face instead of bending at the cheek, and no stray hair strand crosses the ear any more.
- Dreadlocks: the hairline sits higher (a longer-looking face) and the locks pile up higher on the head for more volume.
- Doe eyes ("Rehaugen") are rounder: a taller opening, a fuller lower lid and a slightly larger iris.
- Happy (closed) eyes no longer show lashes. The "light" lashes are now called "natural". Full lashes no longer add lower lashes by themselves: switch on "lower lashes" instead.
- The heart face shape is fuller: the cheeks and jaw are less narrow, so the face no longer looks thin.
- Cat eyes are tilted less, so they look more horizontal.
- The "window light" background now shows the window frame and panes around the head in the portrait view (still soft and blurry).
- The sleeves are set softly into the top: the outline of the rounded sleeve cap fades out towards the chest, so it no longer looks like a seam or a shoulder pad (all tops).
- The shadow of the hair on the clothes now follows the real edge of the hair (long, wavy, curly and dreadlocks) instead of the shoulder line. Short hair casts no shoulder shadow any more.
- The older hairstyles now match the soft painted look: "short and messy" has soft, curved locks instead of hard cartoon spikes, drill curls are smooth glossy spirals, and the buns and the braid got soft shading (the braid now grows out of three strands of hair that go into the first link at about ear height; it is full width from the start and narrows towards the end), fine strands and the warm light edge like the rest of the hair.
- Wavy hair now really looks wavy: soft S-waves in the outline and in the strands.
- The face shape no longer changes the body: shoulders and neck stay the same for round, oval, heart and square faces.
- The heart face shape is nicer: wide cheekbones, a clearly narrower jaw than the oval face and a small, softly rounded chin instead of a pointed one.
- Bangs are softer and less jagged: fewer, wider locks with rounded tips and shallow rounded gaps instead of sharp teeth, and blunt bangs are one clean, smooth line.
- With braids, ponytails, twintails, side ponytails and buns, no hair hangs down at the back any more: the hair is pulled back snugly and only the tail, braid or buns hang down, whatever the hair length.
- The category tabs (body, face, eyes, hair, ...) stay visible at the top while scrolling through the options.
- The anime mode now has a semi-realistic anime look: a slimmer face with a soft chin, smaller almond-shaped eyes with a dark lash line, eyelid crease, detailed iris and soft eyeshadow, a real nose with soft shading, full lips with a gloss highlight, tapered eyebrows, softer shading and finer, lighter outlines everywhere, wispy bangs and loose single hairs, and grown-up body proportions (about seven heads tall). Saved characters keep all their settings and are drawn in the new look.
- More painterly look, especially in the portrait view: warm, rosy shadows on the skin (eye sockets, nose tip, ears, chin) and soft highlights; hair with many fine strands, a light and a shadow side and a warm glow on its outer edges; bang tips that fade out softly; glossier eyes with a wet lower lid and finer, denser lashes; outlines are only half visible.
- New characters start with a defined nose, full lips, an oval face, lighter blush and no ahoge; the dice picks real noses and lips more often.
- Hair is drawn more nicely: more strand lines, a shine band on the crown, a soft darker underside and gentle shading towards the tips.
- New characters start with almond-shaped eyes; saved characters keep their eye shape.
- Bodies can now be made much slimmer: the lower half of the build, shoulders, waist and hips sliders gives a clearly slender figure with narrower torso, thinner arms and legs. Characters at or above the default settings look exactly as before.
- The language now follows the system language automatically (German or English); the manual language switch in the header is gone.
- The portrait view zooms in closer on the face.
- Bangs now end in sharp points with a slight sweep and uneven lengths, instead of looking like round drops.
- Clothes look painted instead of cartoonish: natural, gently sloping shoulders with sleeves that hug the arm (no more puffy sleeves), soft fabric folds, a light and a shadow side, a soft shadow of the chin and hair on the clothes, and very soft collarbones where the neckline is open.
- Back hair ends in soft, pointed, uneven tips that fade out slightly, with a few loose strands, fine strands following the hair flow and a warm glow on its outer edges (instead of round scallops and flat panels).

### Added

- Four new hairstyles: curls (a full mane of spiral ringlets, from a short curly bob to mid-back with the length slider), afro (round and voluminous, the length slider sets its size), dreadlocks (locks from the hairline over the shoulders, with small gold cuffs) and a pixie cut (very short and textured). Afro and dreadlocks bring their own hairline, so the bangs choice is hidden for them. Bows, ears, hats and the ahoge sit on top of the afro, curls and dreadlocks.
- Two new mood backgrounds: "Sunset" (warm evening sky, low sun and soft hazy hills) and "Window light" (a room with soft light falling through a window from the upper left).
- New background "Lights": a mood background with warm light from the upper left and soft, out-of-focus lights around the head.
- More hairstyles: side ponytail, side braid, princess cut, short and messy, and drill curls.
- More bangs: blunt, swept to the left, spiky and open forehead.
- More face shapes: almond, doe and narrow eyes; button, defined and soft-shadow noses; full lips, smirk and wide grin mouths.
- Feedback dialog: a tip that the keyboard's microphone key can be used to dictate instead of typing.
- Feedback button in the header: write a message and share it through the phone's share menu, together with a picture of the character and a settings code that restores it. Without a share menu, the text is copied and the picture is downloaded instead.
- Dark mode: follows the system setting, and a button at the top right switches between light and dark (the choice is remembered).
- Portrait view: a button on the preview zooms in on head and shoulders (and back to the full body), so the face is easy to design at anime proportions. The choice is remembered and used for the PNG export.
- First version of the web app: a cute 2D character builder with an anime mode (layered SVG drawing).
- Proportion slider from chibi to anime, plus height, build, shoulders, waist, hips and skin tone.
- Face, eyes, hair, outfit, accessories and background options, all adjustable with live preview.
- Randomize, reset, undo and redo, save to a gallery (kept in the browser), export as PNG.
- German and English interface, switchable in the header; the choice is remembered.
- Web app manifest and app icon, so the page can be added to a home screen.
- The app is published on GitHub Pages and rebuilt automatically after every change to `main`.

### Fixed

- On phones and tablets, swiping up or down over a slider (e.g. eye spacing) now scrolls the page instead of moving the slider. A slider moves only when dragged sideways or tapped.

### Removed

- The proportion slider (chibi to anime) is gone; every character now uses normal anime proportions. Saved characters that used a chibi setting look different now (taller, smaller head).
